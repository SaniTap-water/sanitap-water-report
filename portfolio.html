Cookstove overlap

17.4% of the carbon hand-pump portfolio — 127 of 731 points — sits in a district where we distributed improved cookstoves, and all of it is in Anosy. The 604 points around Maroantsetra carry no exposure at all.

How the people-served figure is built

WorldPop, not a headcount. Population comes from WorldPop R2025A v1 2025 constrained, the 100 m gridded population surface (hub dataset 74239), pinned by checksum 838c2fc7…. For each water point we take a 1 km service area on a local equidistant projection so the radius is a true 1,000 m; apply the SDWS 1 barrier clip, so a river with no crossing cuts the area; split the population where service areas overlap, so nobody is counted twice; then cap each point at its pump’s capacity — Canzee 500, India Mark 300.

128,221 is the conservative figure and the one in the carbon file. 126,780 of it is the carbon portfolio; the remaining 1,441 is Marolinta, which is separately funded and excluded from every carbon figure.
