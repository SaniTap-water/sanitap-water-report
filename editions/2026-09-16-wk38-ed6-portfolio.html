What this shows

Every asset SaniTap maintains in Madagascar, one marker per asset: 906 hand pumps run with MadAvance in Maroantsetra, Anosy and Androy, and 111 records from the Endur’O register — the piped joint venture with NatuRano — across Analamanga, the Moramanga axis and the south. Hover any marker for its field photograph, its village and its water point number.

Positions and photographs are read live from mWater, so this is the same record a verifier would open. Use the Layers tab to turn a partner or a region on and off.

The rehabilitation of most of these points was already paid for by other funders. Carbon finance does not repeat that capital cost; it funds the servicing that otherwise stops after a few years.

906 hand pumps under management, 839 of them with a field photograph on file, loaded live from the mWater register — the same file a verifier would open. The carbon portfolio is the 804 points in the Data tab; the difference is Marolinta, which is separately funded, and points retired or excluded in the September register reconciliation. Endur’O southern positions come from the MG MERL register where a record matched and the UNICEF site list of 13 February 2026 otherwise; 83 of the 111 carry a photograph. The Moramanga taps are the points sampled for the water quality baseline. Two test records found inside the hand-pump register have been excluded. Population served follows the Gold Standard SDWS 1 method: a 1 km service area per point on the WorldPop raster, overlapping areas split equally, capped at pump capacity.
