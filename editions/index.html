On the estate map in archived editions. The portfolio map reads live from the mWater API. An archived copy preserves the map's markup as issued, but the data it draws is whatever mWater holds when you open it — not the estate as at the issue date. Do not cite an archived map as evidence of the estate at that date; use the figures in the report body, which are frozen.
